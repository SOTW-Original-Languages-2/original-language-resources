# Rights, provenance and attribution

This repository publishes a **selected-source evidence projection**, not the original private research archives or a general license grant covering every source. The authoritative, edition-specific rights declarations and source hashes are in [`study-data/v1/attribution.json`](study-data/v1/attribution.json).

- **Strong’s Hebrew:** selected James Strong Hebrew digital OSIS source; source-declared Public Domain. Complete authoritative selected-source lexical records are hosted in the separate **Part 1** foundation.
- **Strong’s Greek:** selected digital Greek Strong’s XML v1.9, edited by Ulrik Sandborg-Petersen, with source-declared CC0; complete Greek entries are also hosted in Part 1.
- **KJV 1769:** word links derive from the accepted historical tagged KJV evidence; native tagged-word location is not a verified English word-token location.
- **BSB:** native original-language tagged source rows are preserved, subject to the accepted Berean Standard Bible source/public-domain declaration; these source rows are not English word offsets.
- **ASV 1901 and Noah Webster Bible 1833:** supported English associations are identified as KJV-derived and retain the accepted exact token evidence. The independent verse text belongs to each translation’s source foundation.
- **Noah Webster 1828 Dictionary:** independent historical **English** dictionary source, not the 1833 Webster Bible or Strong’s; its separate digital transcription is credited under its specific CC BY-SA 4.0 declaration. Dictionary text is **not** copied into this Part 2 payload.
- **Other commentary / STEP / BDB / Thayer / TWOT:** no such textual definitions or commentary are included here. A referenced source is not permission to reproduce unrelated content.

Each downstream consumer should display the correct edition and witness labels and retain source-specific attribution. No claim is made that every displayed English word has an evidence-backed Strong’s correspondence or that a word’s Strong’s definitions determine the meaning of a particular context. Full research provenance and source-rights decisions remain in the owner-held archives and immutable manifest history.

There is **no blanket `LICENSE`** for every resource in this repository because the components have different source declarations. Do not remove attribution or mislabel native vs. KJV-derived evidence.
